## Platform

web

## Users

Peers, engineering collaborators, and curious visitors exploring Taylor Kaiman's engineering craft, technical history, and personal game review writing.

## Product Purpose

Serve as Taylor Kaiman's personal home on the web (`taylorkaiman.dev`)—uniting a substantive, accurate engineering record with personal critical writing (`/GameReviews`) in one cohesive showcase. Success means a visitor immediately grasps what Taylor builds at scale, how Taylor thinks, and can browse or filter Taylor's written game reviews without dead-end placeholder entries.

## Positioning

A software engineer trained in chemistry who architects planet-scale core platform, AI migration, and global payments infrastructure at Google—paired with candid, searchable critical writing on RPGs and games rather than a sterile corporate resume template.

## Operating Context

Evaluated primarily on desktop and mobile web browsers by fellow software engineers, collaborators, and acquaintances following a link from GitHub (`github.com/tkaiman`), LinkedIn (`linkedin.com/in/taylor-kaiman-4623921a7`), or direct domain visit (`taylorkaiman.dev`). Visitors either read Taylor's background and engineering scope on the home surface or search and filter game reviews by title, body text, and genre tags.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Vite + React + TypeScript single-page web application with client-side routing (`/` engineering overview showcase and `/GameReviews` searchable and genre-filterable game reviews).
- The Overview page (`/`) is dedicated solely to Taylor's engineering career, technical stack, and interactive suminagashi fluid basin; Game Reviews must not be shown or referred to anywhere on `/` except as the single `GAME REVIEWS` navigation link in the left rail.
- `/GameReviews` loads review entries from `/reviews.json`, supports instant text search across title and review body, sorting by rating or title, and multi-genre filtering.
- The 7 placeholder `"Test review"` entries in `public/reviews.json` must be removed or replaced so only real written reviews ship unless new real reviews are provided by Taylor.
- Do not fabricate employers, metrics, testimonials, project claims, or unauthored game reviews.

## Product Principles

1. **Substance over resume filler:** Lead with concrete systems, scale (175+ global partners, network tokenization, millions of daily entries), and real opinions rather than generic self-promotional adjectives.
2. **Two halves of one person:** Treat serious backend/platform engineering and personal game criticism as natural companions within one coherent identity, not disconnected mini-sites.
3. **Zero placeholder rot:** Ship only real, finished writing and verified facts—never lorem ipsum or leftover test rows.
4. **Immediate utility on interactive views:** Filtering, sorting, and searching reviews should feel instantaneous, legible, and self-explanatory at any viewport width.
